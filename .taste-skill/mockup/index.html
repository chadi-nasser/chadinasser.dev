About

I’m Chadi Nasser, a DevOps engineer and software developer based in Lebanon. For 3+ years I’ve built and scaled the backend behind lbauto.trade: Java and Spring services, JWT authentication, request rate limiting, a MongoDB-to-MySQL migration with Flyway, and the Jenkins and Docker pipeline that puts it in production. I studied computer science at the Lebanese International University and finished my master’s with a 3.96 GPA. I like systems that hold up under pressure and pages that load on a bad line. This one ships no JavaScript. Off the clock I read, break my phone with custom kernels, and write about the tools I use.
